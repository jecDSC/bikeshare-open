Exercise 3: run the `leakage-auditor` subagent against a teammate's repo,
report only findings that change a number, and say which of the auditor's
findings I agree with.

Reading it:

- **Tests, reproducibility, row loss: confirmed.** 17 passed;
  `metrics.json` regenerated byte-identical; 728 -> 714, the 14-row bound.
- **"Matches on all 714 rows": confirmed, once checked the right way.** My

  no number changes.

## My verdict

I agree that nothing changes a number. I reran his tests, reproducibility of results, and row loss, which came back confirmed. The feature table check gave 712 rows rather than 714, which I thought strange, until I checked against the raw calendar as the auditor did: 714 of 714. The 2 missing rows came from my first check's method (their previous day had already been dropped from the feature table), not from his feature. The auditor did not mention the raw hash at all. His committed file is correct, but our hashes differ as my Windows checkout added 729 carriage returns, because his repo has no `.gitattributes` rule for `data/raw`. Pandas reads identical values, so there's no number changes. One more thing to note is that his `temp_lag_1` made hgb worse on his validation, but he kept it for ridge's gain. Perhaps it was the better of several choices that he was exploring on his end, but he should've reported it as a mixed result or gave some indicator to the strength of his result relative to his other options.
